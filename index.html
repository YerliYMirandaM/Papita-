Who we are

Who We Are

PetnovaYM is a digital platform designed to help pet owners organize and improve the care of their animals.

It is made for dog and cat owners and the people who care for them: pet profiles, health records, reminders, lost-pet alerts and nearby services in one place.

Our story

Our Story

PetnovaYM is a project by PetNovitasTeam, based in La Chorrera, West Panama.

The idea is simple: caring for a pet should be easier. We want pet information, reminders and safety tools together in one place, available from any phone.

Our mission

Our Mission

To make pet care, health, safety and well-being easier through accessible and practical digital tools.

Our vision

Our Vision

To become a reference platform for responsible pet care in Panama and, later, expand to other countries.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Who we are

PetnovaYM is a project by PetNovitasTeam, based in La Chorrera, West Panama. For any privacy question write to mirandamyerliy@gmail.com.

What this version does

This is a demonstration version. It has no server: the information you enter is saved only in your own browser and is not sent to PetnovaYM.

Information saved in your browser

  • Account details: name, email and profile type. Passwords are never saved as plain text; only a salted hash is kept.
  • Pet profiles, health records, calendar events, alerts and saved favorites.
  • Photos you add, resized in your browser. Location data hidden in a photo is removed when it is resized.

Third-party services

When you visit the site, your browser requests content from outside services: Google Fonts (fonts), cdnjs (map library), OpenStreetMap (map tiles and place data) and Unsplash (example photos). These services receive your IP address and technical data, and the map services also receive the area you are viewing. They have their own privacy policies.

Location

Your position is used only if you press "Near me" in Smart Map. It stays in your browser to sort places by distance and is not stored or sent to PetnovaYM.

Contact

When you use the contact form, your email or WhatsApp app opens with the message ready. The message is then handled by those services.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any item in each tool, log out at any time, or remove everything saved by PetnovaYM in this browser with the button below.

Future changes

When real accounts and a server are added, this policy will be updated, consent will be requested where required, and it will be reviewed against Panama's Law 81 of 2019 on personal data protection.
